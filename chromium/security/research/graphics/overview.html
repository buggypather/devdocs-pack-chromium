Chromium Graphics

Authors: chrome-offsec-team@google.com
Last updated: April 7, 2023

Overview

Chromium graphics features are among the most complex parts of the browser. This document is a snapshot of our evolving understanding of graphics features from an attacker perspective. Relevant and important pieces of the graphics stack are omitted, but the intent is to give VRP contributors a boost when getting started with unfamiliar graphics features.
