Google Play Services in Chrome for Android

Google Play Services is a collection of services exposed on GMS-certified Android devices. Chrome relies on it for features like Sign In, Feedback and Cast.

The list of libraries and versions used by Chrome is listed in //third_party/android_deps/build.gradle. To update this list, refer to its README.md
